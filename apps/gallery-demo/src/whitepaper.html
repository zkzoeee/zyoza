Zyoza: Proof-Carrying Shielded State Objects for Zcash

Abstract. Public blockchains expose the entire ownership graph, price history, and metadata of non-fungible assets. Conversely, Zcash’s shielded protocols—Orchard, Zcash Shielded Assets (ZSA, ZIP 226/227), and Project Tachyon—preserve financial privacy by keeping consensus intentionally lean, omitting on-chain media storage and arbitrary smart-contract state. Zyoza bridges this gap without bloating Zcash L1: encrypted state travels out-of-band (zyoza-oob), lifecycle rules fold into constant-size recursive Ragu non-uniform Proof-Carrying Data (PCD) (zyoza-proofs), and Orchard single-use seals crystallize 1:1 into native ZSA notes and Tachyon bundles.

1. Three Orthogonal Layers (Zero Validator Bloat)

Instead of asking Zcash full nodes to store JPEGs or execute a general-purpose VM, Zyoza separates every digital object into three independent layers:

On Zcash mainnet today, every Orchard action already carries an encrypted 512-byte memo field. Zyoza packs a deterministic single-use seal into those 512 bytes while keeping megabytes of artwork off-chain:

O(1) Bandwidth Transfers via DEK Re-Wrapping

Media payloads are encrypted once under a random 256-bit Data Encryption Key (DEK) using ChaCha20Poly1305 bound to AAD = AssetId. When Alice transfers a 50 MB artwork or deed to Bob, she never re-uploads the file—she unwraps the 32-byte DEK and re-encapsulates only those 32 bytes to Bob’s public key (rewrap_for_recipient).

3. Private Scarcity Without Public totalSupply()

Because shielded mints are hidden, buyers need a cryptographic guarantee that a creator did not silently mint duplicates or exceed a collection cap. Each collection commits to a declarative CollectionSpec(issuer_vk, slug, max_supply, unique_media, mint_window) enforcing two checks:

  1. Deterministic Mint Nullifiers: Every mint atomically reveals domain-separated nullifiers on the settlement layer:
    • NF_aid = BLAKE2b("ZYOZA-MINT-AID1_", col_id || AssetId) — prevents minting the same AssetId twice with a new nonce.
    • NF_idx = BLAKE2b("ZYOZA-MINT-IDX1_", col_id || token_index) — prevents two assets from claiming edition slot i.
    • NF_med = BLAKE2b("ZYOZA-MINT-MED1_", col_id || SHA256(media)) — prevents copy-minting identical artwork inside the collection.
  2. In-Circuit Supply-Cap Range Proof (IssuanceCapEngine): A Halo2 circuit over Pasta Fp decomposes (i - 1) and (N - i) into 16 binary-constrained bits and enforces (i - 1) + 1 + (N - i) = N, proving 1 <= i <= N in zero knowledge without revealing which edition slot was minted.

4. Why Ragu Non-Uniform PCD Beats Indexers & Monolithic zkVMs

Verifying multi-hop asset rules (royalties paid, lockups respected, event check-ins, game upgrades) normally forces an ugly trade-off: public indexers destroy privacy, client-side DAG replay grows linearly O(H) with hop depth and leaks prior holders, and monolithic zkVMs pay worst-case proving latency on every transfer.

Zyoza adopts Ragu’s Non-Uniform Proof-Carrying Data (PCD) over Zcash’s Pasta curves (no trusted setup):

  • Specialized Step Subcircuits: Each transition proves only its active circuit—MintStep, TransferStep, RoyaltyStep (enforcing royalty_req * (1 - royalty_paid) == 0 in-circuit), TicketCheckInStep, or AttributeUpgradeStep.
  • Constant-Size Recursive Lineage: Step k folds the running accumulator into one constant-size proof (2,112 B raw IPA / ~6.2 KB JSON envelope). A buyer verifies a 12-hop cross-profile history in ~16 ms while learning zero prior owners.
  • Five Composable Profiles: Collectibles (NFT1), anonymous event tickets (TKT1), soulbound credentials (CRD1), mutable cypherpunk gear (GAM1), and credential-gated real-world deeds (RWA1).

5. Selective Disclosure & Dark-Pool Sealed-Bid Auctions

Holders derive per-object viewing keys OVK = BLAKE2b("ZYOZA-OVK-v1", AssetId || secret) to reveal a single item’s preview on a marketplace while keeping the rest of their Zcash wallet invisible. Combined with Halo2 ownership and trait proofs (prove_ownership, prove_attribute), sellers list with cryptographic ZK Verified badges and clear sealed-bid ZEC auctions without front-running.
