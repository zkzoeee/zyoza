How Zyoza Works in Three Steps

Plain-English Overview
STEP 01 · SEAL & MINT

Encrypt Once, Anchor a 512-Byte Seal

Artwork and metadata are encrypted off-chain so they never bloat Zcash. Only a compact 512-byte cryptographic seal and deterministic anti-duplicate tags are anchored to the ledger.

STEP 02 · TRADE & FOLD

Pass a 32-Byte Key & Fold the Proof

When an item changes hands, the seller re-wraps only the 32-byte decryption key to the buyer's public key and folds one Ragu proof step—never re-uploading media.

STEP 03 · PROVE OR UPGRADE

Show One Item, Keep Your Wallet Dark

Collectors reveal a single item to a gallery or ticket gate using a per-object viewing key + zero-knowledge Merkle proof, or burn the seal 1:1 into a native ZSA token.

Four Architectural Folds

Explicit Circuit Bindings & Current Prototype Limits
01 · IDENTITY & SCARCITY Implemented (Stage I Indexed)

ZIP 227 AssetDigest & Bound Range Proofs

Derives 64-byte BLAKE2b-512("ZSA-Asset-Digest") + Pallas AssetBase. Ragu's IssuanceCapCircuit binds 1 ≤ slot ≤ cap to (collection_id, asset_id); global uniqueness relies on a public Stage I nullifier log until Stage II ZSA consensus.

02 · OUT-OF-BAND WRAPPING Implemented (PQ: Design Target)

O(1) Pallas ECDH Key Handover

Payloads encrypt once under ChaCha20Poly1305. Transfers re-wrap only the 32-byte DEK via fresh OS-random ephemeral Pallas ECDH + HKDF-SHA256. (Hybrid ML-KEM-768 encapsulation is a future design target.)

03 · RECURSIVE LINEAGE Implemented (See Ragu Caveat)

Ragu Non-Uniform Recursive PCD

Enforces owner-tag authorization, commitment contiguity, sticky RoyaltyCircuit routing + creator receipt preimages, and public ticket nullifiers in a 10-slot header. (Terminal compressed proofs hide witnesses; uncompressed fold state is not yet unlinkable in upstream Ragu.)

04 · SETTLEMENT ADAPTERS Simulated In-Memory Ledger

Orchard Seal → ZSA → Tachyon

Implements ZIP 302 0xFF||"ZYOZ" memo encoding, real orchard address derivation, owner-bound MigrationCircuit burn proofs, and 2-of-3 Pallas Schnorr signing over simulated Orchard, ZSA, and Tachyon ledgers.
