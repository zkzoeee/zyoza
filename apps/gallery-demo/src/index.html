Why Zyoza Works Without Smart-Contract Bloat
A. What Touches Zcash vs. What Travels Out-of-Band

Every Orchard shielded output already includes a 512-byte encrypted memo. Zyoza packs a cryptographic seal into those exact 512 bytes—validators see only a standard shielded note, while encrypted media and Ragu proofs travel peer-to-peer:

"ZYOZ"4B Magic
v00012B Ver
Profile4B Tag
AssetId32B ZIP-227
StateRoot32B SHA256
Nonce32B Salt
OOB Hash32B Bind
OOB Pointer URI374B (Len + URI)

Spending the Orchard note retires the seal. Even if a creator tries to re-mint the same slot or image with a fresh random nonce, deterministic collection nullifiers (BLAKE2b(col_id || slot)) collide and reject the duplicate.
