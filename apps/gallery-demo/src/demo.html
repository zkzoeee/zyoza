Zyoza Studio

Take one object through its whole life in six steps. Every proof here is computed for real by the server, and only the ledger is simulated. Alice, Bob and Carol are demo actors with derived keys.

Step 01 · Mint
Proofs: real · Ledger: simulated Ready

Mint a sealed object

Pick a specimen and a slot. Studio derives its Zcash asset identity, encrypts its state, starts its lineage proof, and proves the slot sits within the collection's cap of 100. The seal and its anti-duplicate tags go to the simulated ledger.

MintCircuit · IssuanceCapCircuit bound to (collection_id, asset_id) · NF_aid / NF_idx / NF_med

Step 02 · Transfer
Key re-wrap + fold: real Locked

Hand it from Alice to Bob

Alice's seal is spent, the 32-byte data key is re-wrapped for Bob, and one step is folded into the proof so Bob becomes the recorded owner. Try decrypting as each actor before and after.

Ephemeral Pallas ECDH + HKDF-SHA256 · ChaCha20-Poly1305 · RoyaltyStep fold updating owner_tag

Step 03 · Fold more steps
10-slot verified header Locked

Add rules from other profiles to the same proof

Each fold checks the current holder's secret, advances the ledger commitment, and re-verifies the whole proof. Fold a ticket check-in twice to see the replay guard, or try the unpaid-royalty attack.

Application::fuse + compress · verify_compressed over all 10 header slots · public SLOT_EVENT_NF

Privacy note: a verifier sees only the compressed proof. Continuing the chain means handing the uncompressed proof to the next owner, which upstream Ragu does not yet make unlinkable.

Step 04 · Disclose & list
Server-issued one-time challenge Locked

Show one object to a gallery

Toggle the per-object viewing key to compare what the server can read with and without it. Then prove you own the object against the gallery's collection tree, using a challenge the server issues once. The wrong-secret attack should fail.

ObjectViewingKey · OwnershipMerkleCircuit bound to (collection_root, state_root, challenge) · committed-trait proof

Step 05 · Sealed-bid auction
Commit: in browser · ZEC clearing: simulated Locked

Commit a bid, reveal it, clear the sale

Your browser hashes the bid with a random 32-byte salt and sends only the hash, so the server cannot see the amount until you reveal it. Clearing hands the object to the winner with a key re-wrap and one more fold.

SHA-256("ZYOZA-BID-CMT-v2" || listing || bidder || amount || salt) · commit → reveal → clear

Step 06 · Crystallize
Proof + signature: real · ZSA ledger: simulated Locked

Burn the seal into a native Zcash asset

Checks the lineage proof against the ledger, proves the current owner authorised the burn, collects a 2-of-3 committee signature, and mints a simulated Stage II ZSA note with the same asset ID. After this, Stage I actions stay locked.

MigrationCircuit bound to owner_tag · 2-of-3 Pallas Schnorr (trusted-dealer demo setup)
